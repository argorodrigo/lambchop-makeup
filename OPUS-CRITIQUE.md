# Second opinion (Claude Opus, acting as MUA) on ChatGPT's three looks

**The short version**
Go with Look B, but change it. ChatGPT picked the right base look and then cut out the one thing that makes Lamb Chop read, which is the droop. Look C would fall apart on your face. Look A is too faint to read from across a room.

**Where ChatGPT goes wrong on your face**
- **The lower fringe in Look C.** Glue holds on lash roots. It doesn't hold on bare under-eye skin that creases every time you smile or talk. Three strips stacked is heavy, so the band will lift at the outer corner within an hour. From a few feet away, lashes hanging toward your cheeks while your eyes are open look like mascara tears or spider legs, not a puppet. Drop Look C.
- **"Keep the outer end level with the eye corner."** Biggest mistake. Lamb Chop's lashes are a crescent that sweeps down and out. Level lashes on open eyes give you glam lashes; with heavy angled brows and stubble that reads as "guy in falsies." Lashes that droop past the corner read as a sleepy puppet.
- **The Y nose seam.** Your moustache sits where the stem of the Y would go. Black lines on the nose tip pick up oil and smudge mid-party. With pink on the tip it reads as a cat. Skip the nose.
- **Pink liner as blush.** Lamb Chop has no blush. Pink pencil on warm medium-brown skin almost disappears when blended; pressed hard it becomes drawn-on dots, and your beard leaves little clear cheek. Use the pink on your mouth instead.
- **"Leave curls visible between the ears."** You have a short fade; dark hair between the ears works against the white wool.
- **Coloring only half the lip** looks like lip color that wore off.

**What makes Lamb Chop recognizable, ranked**
1. Thick black lashes that curve down, on eyes that look closed. Liner and stacked strips can do this.
2. The red open mouth. Pink liner pressed densely is the closest you have.
3. White wool and ears with tan insides. Makeup can't do these.

One crescent of lashes per eye. Lashes on both upper and lower lines read as a doll, not a lamb. Top only, and make them droop.

**Your look (about 20 minutes)**
1. Wash and dry your eyelids. No moisturizer near the eyes.
2. Build two stacks from 4 strips. Hold a strip against the upper lash line from above the inner edge of the iris to about 5 mm past the outer corner; trim the extra from the inner end. Glue a second strip on top with its outer end 1 to 2 mm past the first. Dry 2 minutes.
3. Liner along the upper lash line: thin at the inner corner, about 3 mm thick at the outer corner, then continue about 6 mm past the corner angled DOWN (a reverse cat-eye). ChatGPT said no wing; this downward tail is what makes the droop.
4. Thin line of glue on the band, wait 45 seconds until tacky. Set the middle of the stack on the lash line first, then press the outer end down onto the liner tail so the overhang points down.
5. Go over the band with liner so the strip blends in.
6. Mouth: fill the whole lower lip and the centre of the upper lip with pink, into the corners. Press lips together, second layer in the centre. Nose and cheeks bare.
7. Ears just in front of the hairline, angled out and slightly down.
8. Photos: eyes closed or looking down, face relaxed, lips slightly open. Eyes closed, the stacked lashes make the exact puppet crescent.

**Your brow idea: drop it.** Lamb Chop has no brows. Strips on them make them heavier, sit too high to look sleepy, and glue in brow hair hurts coming out. One strip on the brow tail looks like a smudge.

**What the costume has to carry.** Makeup gives the eyes and mouth. The white wool, the ears and the silhouette must come from clothes: white from the neck down and, if possible, a white hood or hat over the dark hair. Without that, people see a guy with dramatic lashes and ears.
