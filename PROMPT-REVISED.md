## Corrections to your plan

1. Drop Look C entirely. Lash glue holds on lash roots, not on bare under-eye skin that creases when I talk or smile. Three stacked strips are heavy and will lift at the outer corner within an hour, and with my eyes open they read as spider legs or mascara tears, not a puppet. Replace it with something that can actually survive a party.
2. "Keep the outer end level with the eye corner" is the biggest mistake. Lamb Chop's lashes are one crescent per eye that sweeps DOWN and out. Level lashes on open eyes, on a face with heavy angled brows and stubble, read as "guy in falsies." The outer end of the stacked strips must droop past the corner.
3. Add a downward liner tail: continue the upper liner about 6 mm past the outer corner, angled down along where the lower lid would continue, a reverse cat-eye. You said no wing; the droop is the entire point of the character, so put it back.
4. Kill the Y nose seam. My moustache sits exactly where the stem goes, black on an oily nose tip smudges by mid-party, and with pink on the tip it reads as a cat. Nose stays bare.
5. Kill the pink blush. Lamb Chop has no blush. Pink pencil on warm medium-brown skin either disappears when blended or turns into drawn-on dots if pressed, and my beard leaves almost no clear cheek. Spend the pink on the mouth instead: whole lower lip plus the centre of the upper lip, pressed dense, into the corners. Do not color half a lip; that looks like worn-off lipstick.
6. "Leave curls visible between the ears" does not apply. I have a short fade. Dark hair between the ears fights the white wool, so suggest how the costume covers it (white hood, hat, or hair wrap) instead.
7. Lashes go on the upper line only. Upper plus lower reads as a doll, not a lamb.
8. The brow idea is dead. Do not revisit it.
