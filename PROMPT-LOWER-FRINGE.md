## The lower fringe, corrected

- Band placement: NOT 3 mm below on bare skin (the soft hollow under the lid is where glue fails first). Glue the band right up against the lower lash roots so it sits on the lash hairs and the firm rim of the lid.
- Stack 2 strips per eye, not 3. Glue the two together off the face, offset about 1 mm, and let them dry into one band. Flip them so the fibers point down; if the curl bends toward the cheek, swap left and right strips so it curls away from the skin.
- Trim fibers to roughly half length (5 to 7 mm), snipping up into the ends, not straight across.
- Use the outer two thirds only, starting above the inner edge of the iris. The inner corner moves too much to hold a band.
- Under the eye: a smudged black pencil crescent along the lower lash line, thickest under the iris, tapered to a point at both ends, carried into the inner corner where there is no band. This fills the gaps between fibers so the eye reads as one solid downward crescent, not spider legs.
- Upper lid: a thin black tightline along the upper lash roots only. No wing, no upper strip, upper lashes bare. One crescent per eye.
- Adhesion: clean, fully dry eye area, no moisturizer. Pre-curve the band around a finger. Thin line of glue, wait 30 to 45 s until tacky. Look up at the ceiling so the lower lid lies flat, press the centre first, then the outer end, hold with a cotton bud 30 s. Once dry, a second hair-thin line of glue along the seam, then liquid liner over the band to hide and seal it (pencil won't grip on glue). Smile with the mouth, not the eyes.
